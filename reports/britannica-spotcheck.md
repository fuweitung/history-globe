# Second-source spot-check: Encyclopaedia Britannica

Checked 6 Nov 2026 by reading britannica.com articles.

| Civilisation | Result |
|---|---|
| Srivijaya | **Differs on the end.** Britannica: flourished 7th–13th century; Chola seized Palembang in 1025; reduced to a small kingdom by the end of the 12th century. This app ends it at 1025 (following Wikipedia). Now marked *disputed* with both views. |
| Majapahit | **Agrees.** 13th–16th century, founder Vijaya (Raden Wijaya), peak under Hayam Wuruk and Gajah Mada, decline after Gajah Mada’s death (1364). Britannica notes scholars disagree on how far its territory reached. |
| Mali | **Mostly agrees.** Founded by Sundiata c. 1235, peak under Mansa Musa; Britannica says it lasted to the 16th century, this app to 1610. Marked *disputed*. |
| Khmer Empire | **Agrees.** About 802–1431, capital Angkor, peak 11th–13th centuries. |

## Why only four

Britannica refuses automated fetches (HTTP 403), and reading it through a browser at scale counts as getting around that block, so the check stopped after four articles. The other 20 major civilisations on the list (Rome, Han, Ottoman, Mughal, Inca, Aztec, Mongol, Abbasid, Byzantium, Tang, Song, Ming, Qing, Maurya, Gupta, Achaemenid, Sasanian, Carthage, Assyria, Melaka) still need a person to spot-check them.
