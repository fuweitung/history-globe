# Stories plan

_Written 14 Nov 2026. The **list of stories** is agreed as a plan. **How to present them is still brainstorming**, so the Stories button stays hidden (`STORIES_ENABLED = false` in `src/App.tsx`)._

## What is decided

- **Core stories:** 38 of the 122 candidates in [IMPROVEMENT-PLAN.md](IMPROVEMENT-PLAN.md) (Part G) are marked core (★). Eight already exist; 30 would be new.
- **First wave (12), all from the core list:**
  1. Out of Africa
  2. The farming revolution
  3. Rome: rise and fall (existing)
  4. Writing to the internet
  5. The spread of the world religions
  6. Medicine
  7. Road to space
  8. Age of Exploration (existing)
  9. The Silk Road (existing)
  10. Democracy and rights
  11. Pandemics
  12. The world wars (existing)
- The other seven existing stories (First civilisations, China’s dynastic cycle, Southeast Asia, Mongols, Islamic golden age, African kingdoms, Colonialism and independence) wait until after the first wave.

## Where the data stands (14 Nov 2026)

- The 11 existing stories have 99 steps, and **none cites a source**.
- They link to 62 events; only 18 of those events have a source and 14 say “(checked …)”. They link to 32 civilisations (57% of those checklist items are checked), 7 people and 8 routes.
- Thin areas for the first wave: **Road to space** has 4 events and 1 person (Galileo’s telescope, Sputnik 1, first human in space, Apollo 11; Gagarin). Medicine, farming and communication have only about 11–15 events each.

## How each story will be built

1. Add the missing sourced events and people the story needs.
2. Check every step against a page that was actually read, and cite it on the step.
3. Only then show the story. One pilot story first, the rest once the pilot is approved.
